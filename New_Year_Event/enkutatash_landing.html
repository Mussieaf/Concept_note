Our Vision

Africa's Most Prestigious Cultural Gathering

A Global Gathering of Ethiopians and Friends of Ethiopia

To establish Ethiopia's New Year Celebration as Africa's most prestigious cultural, tourism, and diaspora gathering, showcasing Ethiopia's rich heritage, investment opportunities, hospitality, arts, cuisine, innovation, and global influence.

Positioned at the iconic National Palace in Addis Ababa, Enkutatash 2019 E.C. transforms a local tradition into a world-class destination celebration comparable to New Year's Eve in Dubai, Rio Carnival, and the Edinburgh Festival.

Unforgettable Experiences

From imperial ceremonies to world-class entertainment

Imperial Opening Ceremony

National Palace ceremonial reception with distinguished guests, red-carpet welcome, traditional horse procession, and military ceremonial band.

World-Class Concerts

International headliners with African heritage appeal, Ethiopian legends, symphony orchestra, children's choir, and drone light shows depicting Ethiopian history.

Ethiopian Cultural Village

Regional pavilions representing all states with traditional houses, indigenous foods, crafts, music, storytelling, and authentic cultural performances.

Grand Food Festival

Heritage cuisine pavilion, coffee experience center, organic food showcase, international cuisine, chef competitions, and culinary master classes.

Fashion & Heritage Showcase

Ethiopian Fashion Week at the Palace featuring traditional attire exhibitions, modern designers, diaspora fashion houses, and jewelry exhibitions.

Coffee & Wine Experience

World's largest coffee ceremony, coffee history museum, tasting experiences, exporters showcase, international buyers forum, and Ethiopian wine tasting.

Investment & Diaspora Forum

Forums on Real Estate, Tourism, Technology, Agriculture, Renewable Energy, and Manufacturing. Connect with diaspora investors, development banks, and venture capital.

Palace Gala Dinners

Exclusive VIP gala with heads of missions, diplomats, diaspora business leaders, international investors, and celebrity guests. Five-course Ethiopian fusion cuisine.

Closing Spectacular

Closing spectacular finale program with Symphony orchestra, National cultural showcase, Fireworks synchronized to Ethiopian music, Countdown to the New Year.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about attending Enkutatash 2019 E.C.

Complete the registration form on this page by selecting your ticket package. After submission, you will receive an email with payment instructions via Bank of Abyssinia. Your registration is confirmed upon payment verification. Digital tickets and QR codes will be sent via email.

Yes, dedicated parking zones (A through D) are available at the National Palace grounds. VIP ticket holders have access to premium parking near the entrance. Standard parking is available on a first-come, first-served basis. We recommend using the free event shuttle from partner hotels.

Business casual for daytime events. Traditional Ethiopian attire is encouraged and celebrated. Evening gala events require formal or cocktail attire. VIP Palace dinners require black tie or traditional formal dress. Comfortable footwear is recommended for the Cultural Village and exhibition areas.

Absolutely! Enkutatash 2019 E.C. welcomes guests from all nations. We have dedicated support for international visitors including visa facilitation, airport transfers, hotel partnerships, and multilingual staff. The event features English and Amharic programming with live translation for major sessions.

Yes, children are welcome! We have a dedicated Children's Village with supervised activities, cultural education, and entertainment. Children under 5 enter free. Family tickets are available at discounted rates. Child safety protocols and identification wristbands are provided at the entrance.

Tickets are refundable up to 30 days before the event (before August 6, 2026) with a 15% processing fee. Between 30-14 days, 50% refund is available. Within 14 days of the event, tickets are non-refundable but transferable to another attendee with prior notification.

Payment is accepted via Bank of Abyssinia transfer in USD or ETB. International wire transfers, Telebirr mobile payment, and major credit cards (at the venue) are accepted. Detailed payment instructions are sent via email upon registration. Corporate sponsors may arrange invoice-based payment.

Yes! Major sessions, ceremonies, and speeches feature simultaneous translation between English and Amharic. Headsets are available at the entrance for international guests. Selected sessions also offer French and Arabic translation. Virtual attendees receive subtitled streams.
