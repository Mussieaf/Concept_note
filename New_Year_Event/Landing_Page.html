Our Vision

Africa's Most Prestigious Cultural Gathering

A Global Gathering of Ethiopians and Friends of Ethiopia

To establish Ethiopia's New Year Celebration as Africa's most prestigious cultural, tourism, and diaspora gathering, showcasing Ethiopia's rich heritage, investment opportunities, hospitality, arts, cuisine, innovation, and global influence.

Positioned at the iconic National Palace in Addis Ababa, Enkutatash 2019 E.C. transforms a local tradition into a world-class destination celebration comparable to New Year's Eve in Dubai, Rio Carnival, and the Edinburgh Festival.

Unforgettable Experiences

From imperial ceremonies to world-class entertainment

Imperial Opening Ceremony

National Palace ceremonial reception with distinguished guests, red-carpet welcome, traditional horse procession, and military ceremonial band.

World-Class Concerts

International headliners with African heritage appeal, Ethiopian legends, symphony orchestra, children's choir, and drone light shows depicting Ethiopian history.

Ethiopian Cultural Village

Regional pavilions representing all states with traditional houses, indigenous foods, crafts, music, storytelling, and authentic cultural performances.

Grand Food Festival

Heritage cuisine pavilion, coffee experience center, organic food showcase, international cuisine, chef competitions, and culinary master classes.

Fashion & Heritage Showcase

Ethiopian Fashion Week at the Palace featuring traditional attire exhibitions, modern designers, diaspora fashion houses, and jewelry exhibitions.

Coffee & Wine Experience

World's largest coffee ceremony, coffee history museum, tasting experiences, exporters showcase, international buyers forum, and Ethiopian wine tasting.

Investment & Diaspora Forum

Forums on Real Estate, Tourism, Technology, Agriculture, Renewable Energy, and Manufacturing. Connect with diaspora investors, development banks, and venture capital.

Palace Gala Dinners

Exclusive VIP gala with heads of missions, diplomats, diaspora business leaders, international investors, and celebrity guests. Five-course Ethiopian fusion cuisine.
