The Strategic Context

The Forum unites three critical pathways to achieve the Climate-Resilient Green Economy (CRGE) strategy.

🌳

The Green Legacy Model

Showcasing Ethiopia's mass mobilization for land restoration as a replicable model for carbon sequestration and rural livelihoods across Africa.

⚛️

Technology Leapfrog

Urgent adoption of Globo Hydro Power’s Hydrogen-Enhanced Internal Combustion Engine (H2-EICE) to decarbonize transport and industry.

🍳

Immediate Relief

Scaling Improved Cooking Stoves and Solar Devices to reduce indoor air pollution and empower women economically.
