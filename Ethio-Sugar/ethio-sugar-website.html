Who We Are

A Private Champion of Ethiopia's Sugar Privatization

Established in 2018 as a private share company, Ethio Sugar Manufacturing Industry S.C. was formed to seize one of Africa's most compelling agribusiness opportunities: the privatization of Ethiopia's state-owned sugar complexes. Headquartered on Africa Avenue in Addis Ababa's Bole Sub-city, we pair deep local insight with modern agricultural and industrial expertise.

🏭

Acquire & Modernize

Wonji/Shoa · Metehara

Strategic participation in the privatization of flagship complexes — bringing fresh capital, modern technology and disciplined management to Ethiopia's most storied sugar estates.

🌱

Greenfield Development

Hamer · Omo Zone

Acquisition of an agro-processing plant and 10,000 hectares of prime land in Hamer, Omo Zone, dedicated to sugarcane cultivation and value-added processing.

Community & Jobs

The Bigger Picture

Ethiopia's Sugar Sector: A National Priority

The state-owned Ethiopian Sugar Industry Group (ESIG) manages the country's major factories — Wonji Shoa, Metehara, Finchaa and Kessem. National production goals and privatization initiatives are opening the door for private investors to bridge Ethiopia's sugar deficit and power industrial growth.

🏛️

ESIG Oversight

State Industry Group

Ethiopian Sugar Industry Group manages major operational factories including Wonji Shoa, Metehara, Finchaa and Kessem.

🎯

Deficit Reduction

Import Substitution

Privatization and development programs aim to close the national sugar gap and replace imports with homegrown output.
