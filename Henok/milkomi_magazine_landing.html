Our Story

Rooted in Oromia,
Growing for Ethiopia

Nestled in the fertile Oromia region, just 107 kilometers east of Addis Ababa, Milkomi Flour Factory has delivered exceptional wheat and corn flour since 2024. Our state-of-the-art facility combines advanced milling technology with deep relationships with local farmers.

Now, we are expanding into HDPE and PP woven fabric production — a strategic diversification driven by growing demand across agriculture, packaging, and construction sectors. By leveraging our existing infrastructure and market position, we aim to capture significant market share while strengthening Ethiopia's manufacturing base.

Product Portfolio

Woven for Every Purpose

From agricultural packaging to civil engineering geotextiles, our product line addresses Ethiopia's most pressing industrial material needs.

HDPE Woven Fabrics

High tensile strength fabrics for crop packaging, storage sacks, ground covers, and greenhouse shading nets with UV stabilization.

PP Woven Fabrics

Lightweight yet durable solutions for cement bags, fertilizers, chemicals, and food products — available in laminated and non-laminated finishes.

Geotextiles

Woven and non-woven configurations for erosion control, soil stabilization, road construction, and civil engineering projects.

Specialty Industrial

Custom-engineered fabrics with flame retardancy, anti-static properties, and chemical resistance for automotive and industrial applications.

Implementation

The Road Ahead

A structured 20-month journey from project inception to full-scale production and market launch.

Months 1–3

Project Planning

Finalize project charter, conduct feasibility studies, secure funding, and develop risk assessment and mitigation plans.

Months 4–6

Design & Development

Facility design, equipment selection and procurement, and technology integration planning for extrusion and weaving lines.

Months 7–11

Construction & Installation

Facility construction, machinery installation, calibration, and initial commissioning of production equipment.

Months 12–16

Pilot Production

Conduct pilot runs of HDPE and PP woven fabrics, perform quality assurance testing, and refine processes based on results.

Months 17–20

Full-Scale Launch

Ramp up to full production capacity, launch marketing and sales campaigns, and distribute products across Ethiopian markets.
