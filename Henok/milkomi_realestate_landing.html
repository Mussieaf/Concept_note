Editorial

The story behind the skyline

Ethiopia's urban economy demands world-class infrastructure. Milkomi Trading Plc answers that call with a landmark development designed for diplomats, investors, and families.

The current fast and dynamic economic growth of Ethiopia, especially in urban areas, necessitates equivalent expansion in the building and construction sector. Property investment has remained underdeveloped for decades, leaving the supply of residential and commercial real estate disproportionately low against surging demand.

Addis Ababa, the diplomatic capital of Africa and host to more than 100 embassies, requires facilities that match its international stature. The city is fast becoming a destination for conference tourism, yet lacks integrated venues combining retail, hospitality, and residential functions under one roof.

Milkomi Trading Plc proposes to fill this gap with a modern 3B+G+15 mixed-use complex on a 10,000 m² plot in central Addis Ababa. Shops, supermarkets, banks, restaurants, a cinema, game zones, cafés, and luxury apartments — all designed to international standards with sustainability at their core.

The CBE Tower — Africa's tallest building, symbolizing Addis Ababa's rapid modernization
Environment

Built for tomorrow

Sustainability is woven into the foundation of the Milkomi complex through renewable energy, waste management, and biophilic design.

Solar energy

Extensive solar panel integration across rooftops and terraces to reduce grid dependency and lower the building's lifetime carbon footprint.

Waste management

Closed-loop wastewater collection via septic tanks and organized solid waste disposal to municipal sites, ensuring zero contamination.

Green spaces

Recreation areas, children's playgrounds, elder walkways, and landscaped parks that improve air quality and community wellbeing.

"As much as possible, solar energy will be utilized after completion. The building will also be glazed with standard glasses. Thus, the project will have no negative impact on the environment."

— Environmental Impact Assessment, Milkomi Project Proposal, September 2024

Spaces

Every floor, a new experience

From underground parking to rooftop amenities, the Milkomi complex is designed as a self-contained urban ecosystem.

Retail haven

Ground to third floors host shops, supermarkets, galleries, and African-flavored specialty stores with open atriums.

Residential sanctuary

Floors 5 through 13 offer two and three-bedroom luxury apartments for diaspora investors and local families.

Business hub

Dedicated floors for banks, insurance firms, and flexible offices serving the diplomatic community and NGOs.

Dining & leisure

Restaurants, coffee houses, a cinema, game zones, and cultural center attracting transit tourists and residents.

Smart parking

Four basement levels accommodate vehicles and generator infrastructure with efficient traffic flow design.

Community wellness

Green recreation areas, children's playgrounds, elder walkways, and assembly halls foster social cohesion.
