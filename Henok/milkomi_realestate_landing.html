Sustainable Development

Building Responsibly.
Building for Generations.

The Milkomi complex integrates environmental stewardship into every design decision. Solar energy utilization, standard glazing for thermal efficiency, and comprehensive waste management systems ensure the building leaves a light footprint.

Wastewater is collected in septic tanks; solid waste is systematically disposed to municipal sites. Green areas and circulation zones occupy the remaining 3,000 m² of the plot, creating breathable urban space.
