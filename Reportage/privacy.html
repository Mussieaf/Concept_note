Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 21, 2026 · Marketing landing page

This Privacy Policy explains how the Reportage Towers landing page handles information submitted through the page, WhatsApp links, email links, forms, chat widgets, and related campaign interactions. By using the page, you agree to the practices described here. This page is a marketing page; final project, price, availability, and purchase terms must be confirmed in writing with the sales team.

1. Information we collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Contact details: name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, and current location.
  • Buyer preferences: unit type, purpose, purchase timeline, budget signals, and requested materials.
  • Messages sent through WhatsApp, email, forms, chatbots, or social campaign links.
  • Technical data: device type, browser, approximate location, pages viewed, buttons clicked, and campaign source/UTM parameters.

2. How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and provide project information.
  • To qualify buyer intent and route hot, warm, or nurture leads to the appropriate sales process.
  • To send requested availability, floor plans, payment guidance, investment briefs, and viewing invitations.
  • To measure advertising performance, improve landing-page usability, and reduce irrelevant follow-up.

3. WhatsApp, email, and messaging

Buttons on this page may open WhatsApp with pre-filled text. If you send a message, you are communicating directly through WhatsApp and your message will be handled under WhatsApp/Meta terms as well as this policy. Email links may open your email client; if you email us, we will use your message to respond to your request.

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with service providers who help operate the website, forms, analytics, advertising measurement, CRM, or customer messaging, and with project sales personnel acting on behalf of the campaign. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety, and fraud prevention.

The page may use cookies, pixels, tags, and similar technologies for functionality, security, analytics, and ad measurement. You can control cookies through your browser settings. If advertising pixels are enabled, they may associate page visits with campaign interactions; where required, consent tools should be used.

We keep enquiry information only as long as needed for sales follow-up, legal, accounting, security, and campaign-measurement purposes. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system is guaranteed to be perfectly secure.

8. Your choices

You may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your personal information, and you may opt out of non-essential marketing messages. To make a request, contact the Buyer Desk below. Some records may be retained where required for legal or legitimate business purposes.
