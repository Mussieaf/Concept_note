The Project

A New Landmark for Addis Ababa

Reportage Towers rises directly opposite the African Union Headquarters in the heart of Addis Ababa — a defining mixed-use development of two contemporary residential towers above a shared retail podium.

Delivered through a strategic partnership between Reportage Ethiopia and the Ethiopian Defence Army Foundation, under the guidance of the Ministry of National Defence, this project represents a new standard for urban living in Ethiopia's capital.

With 1,086 meticulously designed residences, a full shopping mall, and a rooftop luxury amenity deck, Reportage Towers is poised to become a model for future international investment and stronger investor confidence in Ethiopia.
