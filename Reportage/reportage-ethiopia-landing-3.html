The Project

Where Contemporary Living Meets Diplomatic Prestige

Reportage Towers rises directly opposite the African Union Headquarters in the heart of Addis Ababa - a landmark mixed-use development of two contemporary residential towers above a shared retail podium. Delivered through a strategic partnership between Reportage Properties and the Ethiopian Defence Army Foundation.

Basement, Ground, and two podium levels support 29 upper floors reaching 110 metres, all resting on a 25,979 square metre plot. The podium houses a full shopping mall, while its rooftop transforms into a luxury amenity deck - a private sanctuary above the city.

"We will be an Ethiopian company operating in Ethiopia." -- Andrea Nucera, CEO & Group Managing Director, Reportage Properties

The Developer

A Global Developer with Local Commitment

Founded in 2014 and headquartered in Abu Dhabi, Reportage Properties operates across 25 countries with a portfolio spanning the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Turkey, Pakistan, Nigeria, Rwanda, Uganda, Kenya and beyond.

In Ethiopia, Reportage has already established a team of over 70 professionals, working with the Ethiopian Defence Army Foundation to set a new standard for residential development in East Africa.

"Ethiopia has tremendous potential. We are committed to supporting the country's growth through developments that create value and opportunities."
