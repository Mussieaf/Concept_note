The Project

Where Contemporary Living Meets Diplomatic Prestige

Reportage Towers rises directly opposite the African Union Headquarters in the heart of Addis Ababa — a landmark mixed-use development of two contemporary residential towers above a shared retail podium. Delivered through a strategic partnership between Reportage Properties and the Ethiopian Defence Army Foundation, under the guidance of the Ministry of National Defence.

Basement, Ground, and two podium levels support 29 upper floors reaching 110 metres, all resting on a 25,979 square metre plot. The podium houses a full shopping mall, while its rooftop transforms into a luxury amenity deck — a private sanctuary above the city.

"We will be an Ethiopian company operating in Ethiopia." — Andrea Nucera, CEO & Group Managing Director, Reportage Properties

The Developer

A Global Developer with Local Commitment

Founded in 2014 and headquartered in Abu Dhabi, Reportage Properties has grown into one of the most dynamic international real estate developers. With a presence spanning 25 countries and a portfolio of residential and mixed-use developments across the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Turkey, Pakistan, Nigeria, Rwanda, Uganda, Kenya, and beyond — the group brings world-class expertise to every market it enters.

In Ethiopia, Reportage has already established a team of over 70 professionals, working hand-in-hand with the Ethiopian Defence Army Foundation to deliver a project that sets a new standard for residential development in East Africa.

"Ethiopia has tremendous potential. We are committed to supporting the country's growth through developments that create value and opportunities."
