01 Company History

stePAP Pharmaceuticals PLC is a legal entity established by pathologists, histotechnologists, and business experts. Recognizing the critical lack of awareness and supply issues in the field of anatomic pathology equipment and consumables across Africa, the company was founded to provide uninterrupted access to high-quality products at affordable prices.

Founded in 2023 and headquartered in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, stePAP is committed to bridging healthcare infrastructure gaps and empowering cancer diagnostic centers with the tools they need to save lives.
