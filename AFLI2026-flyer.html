About The Forum

A Groundbreaking Summit for Africa's Future

The African First Ladies Initiative for Emission Reduction and Poverty Alleviation Forum (AFLI-ERPA) is a high-level forum bringing together First Ladies, global investors, and stakeholders to accelerate climate action and economic empowerment.

Building on the success of "Green Legacy Initative".

Forum Objectives

Green Legacy Initiative

Accelerate emission reduction strategies with women as key agents in planting trees.

Women Economic Empowerment

Drive poverty alleviation through women green entrepreneurship.

Clean Energy Solutions

Advance renewable energy access for rural communities.

Global Partnerships

Policy Influence
