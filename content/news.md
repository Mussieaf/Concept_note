# 🌍 Latest Forum Updates

## March 2026 Summit Announced
The African First Ladies Initiative for Emission Reduction and Poverty Alleviation Forum will convene on **March 17-19, 2026** at The National Palace in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia.

## Key Highlights

### 🌱 Green Legacy Milestone
Ethiopia's transformative Green Legacy Initiative has now planted over **47.5 billion seedlings**, creating unprecedented green economic opportunities and environmental resilience across the nation.

### ⚡ Technology Showcase
This year's forum will feature groundbreaking clean energy technologies:

- **Hydrogen-Enhanced Internal Combustion Engines (H-ICE)**
- **Advanced Solar Power Devices**
- **Improved Clean Cooking Stoves**

### 🤝 Partnership Opportunities
We welcome sponsors and partners committed to:
- Climate-smart development
- Women and youth empowerment
- Sustainable economic growth
