Climate Summit 📅 January 2025

🌍 Ethiopia Wins Bid to Host COP32 in 2027, Edging Out Nigeria

Ethiopia has been selected to host the 32nd Conference of the Parties (COP32) to the UN Framework Convention on Climate Change in 2027, beating Nigeria in the final round of voting. This landmark decision positions Ethiopia as a leading voice in African climate diplomacy and underscores the international community's recognition of the country's ambitious green initiatives, including the Green Legacy Initiative.

The conference is expected to draw over 50,000 delegates and generate significant momentum for climate finance commitments to African renewable energy projects. Ethiopia's successful bid highlights its growing influence in shaping continental environmental policy.

Reforestation 📅 December 2024

🌱 Ethiopia's Green Legacy Initiative Surpasses 50 Billion Tree Planting Milestone

Ethiopia's ambitious Green Legacy Initiative has reached an extraordinary milestone, with over 50 billion trees planted since the program's launch in 2019. The initiative, spearheaded by the Office of the First Lady, has become Africa's largest reforestation effort and a model for continental ecological restoration.

Recent satellite imagery confirms that approximately 15% of these plantings have matured into established forest cover, significantly enhancing carbon sequestration capacity and restoring degraded watersheds. The program has created over 750,000 green jobs, with 60% held by women and youth.

Clean Technology 📅 November 2024

⚡ Breakthrough Reduces Harmful Emissions from Hydrogen Engines

Researchers at the Addis Ababa Institute of Technology, in partnership with international collaborators, have announced a significant breakthrough in hydrogen-enhanced internal combustion engine (H-ICE) technology. The new system reduces nitrogen oxide emissions by 85% while improving fuel efficiency by 40%.

This innovation is particularly significant for African nations seeking to retrofit existing vehicle fleets and power generation equipment. The technology will be showcased at the AFLI 2026 Forum, with pilot projects planned for public transport systems in Addis Ababa and Nairobi.

Renewable Energy 📅 October 2024

👩‍🌾 Ethiopia Inaugurates Landmark Aysha-II Wind Power Project

Ethiopia has officially commissioned the Aysha-II Wind Power Project, a 300-megawatt facility located in the Somali Regional State near the Djibouti border. The $450 million project, funded through a consortium of international development banks, represents the largest wind energy installation in East Africa.

The project incorporates innovative battery storage technology and is expected to power approximately 3 million households. Notably, 40% of the engineering and technical positions are held by women, setting a new standard for gender inclusion in African energy infrastructure.

Solar Energy 📅 September 2024

☀️ Solar Mini-grids Projects in Ethiopia - Solar Financed Africa

The Solar Financed Africa initiative has launched a $200 million program to deploy solar mini-grids across 200 rural Ethiopian communities. The project combines innovative pay-as-you-go financing with community ownership models, ensuring sustainable long-term operation.

Each mini-grid installation includes capacity for agricultural processing equipment, enabling rural cooperatives to add value to their produce. The program targets communities currently without grid electricity, prioritizing areas with high concentrations of women-led agricultural enterprises.

Clean Cooking 📅 August 2024

🔥 Clean Cooking Alternative Financing Project Launched in Addis Ababa

The Ministry of Finance, in partnership with the World Bank and African Development Bank, has launched a $150 million Clean Cooking Alternative Financing Project. The initiative aims to distribute 2 million improved cookstoves and 500,000 biogas systems over the next three years.

The project introduces an innovative results-based financing mechanism where manufacturers receive payments based on verified health and environmental outcomes. This approach has already attracted 12 local manufacturing companies, creating a domestic clean cooking industry expected to employ 5,000 people.

Carbon Credit 📅 26 Feb 2026

🔥 UN approves first carbon credits under Paris Agreement market mechanism

Paris Agreement’s carbon credits enable cross-border trade to support emissions reduction and climate goals worldwide.

The United Nations has approved the first credits to be issued under a carbon market established by the Paris climate accord, aimed at reducing emissions – a mechanism that has faced scrutiny over greenwashing concerns. The UN-run market allows companies and countries to offset their excess emissions by financing projects that cut greenhouse gases in other nations.
