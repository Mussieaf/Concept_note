01 — The Story

The world's most electric-friendly policy frontier

Ethiopia has emerged as Africa's boldest electric-mobility laboratory. With combustion-engine vehicle imports restricted and national climate commitments anchored in the Climate-Resilient Green Economy (CRGE) strategy and the Ten-Year Development Plan, the country is building an almost exclusively electric vehicle fleet — yet public charging infrastructure remains the critical missing link.

Whoever builds the charging network, owns the artery of Ethiopia's electric transport economy.

This program converts that gap into a structured, bankable opportunity: a ring-fenced Ethiopian special-purpose vehicle (SPV), financed by overseas pension-fund debt, supplied exclusively by a Chinese charging-equipment manufacturer, and anchored by a local operator holding land, permits and grid relationships.

02 — The Program

A three-phase national build-out

Deployment is deliberately staged: prove the model in the capital, scale along proven corridors, then industrialize.

  • Phase I (2026–2027): 220–320 selected fuel stations & public service sites across the Addis Ababa, Adama and Hawassa agglomerations and inter-regional trunk highways; 900–1,400 charging points with ≥60% DC fast charging. Addis Ababa pilot stations commissioned Q4 2026; national roll-out through 2027.
  • Phase II (2028–2030): 130–210 dedicated charging stations at malls, industrial parks, residential compounds and national highway service areas, with mixed DC/AC configuration matched to site power supply.
  • Long-term (2031–2035): local charging-pile assembly line inside Ethiopian industrial parks; energy-storage second-life and V2G demonstrations; replication to selected neighboring markets scaled by population, vehicle stock and grid capacity.

04 — Deal Architecture

The ring-fenced SPV at the center

A jointly registered Ethiopian special-purpose vehicle (preferred form: PLC / Private Limited Company under the Ethiopian Commercial Code) holds all charging and user data, signs all site and grid agreements, and is the exclusive borrower of pension-fund debt.

Party A — Local Operator

Ethiopian Anchor Partner

Contributes land, site resources, operating permits, government liaison (Ethiopian Investment Commission, Ministry of Water & Energy, regional administrations) and grid-access coordination via Ethiopian Electric Utility. Solely responsible for meeting lender eligibility thresholds.

Party B — Equipment & Technology Supplier

Chinese Manufacturer

Contributes core charging technology, IP, know-how and warranty-service commitment as equity — no cash injection. SPV procures all hardware exclusively from Party B under market-priced commercial contracts, GB/T + CCS-2 dual interface.

Party C — Cross-Border Coordinator

Capital & Compliance Bridge

Structures the financing, introduces overseas pension-fund lenders, coordinates cross-border remittance and profit repatriation under National Bank of Ethiopia forex rules and AML law. No capital contribution, no project risk.

Step dividends lock permanently once Party A's investment principal is recovered, certified by a jointly appointed Ethiopian audit firm.
