Cooperation Model

Three-Party Joint Venture Structure

A special purpose vehicle (SPV) jointly established to oversee investment, construction, and nationwide operation of charging infrastructure across Ethiopia.

Party A

Ethiopian Local Operator

Holds franchise rights for the project, fully bears all capital investment, and manages land formalities, government approvals, and local operations across Ethiopia.

Full cash contribution ($10M+ registered capital)
Bears operational risks jointly with Party B
67.5% dividends pre-payback / 47.5% post-payback
Party B

Chinese Equipment & Tech Supplier

Leading Chinese EV charging manufacturer providing full industrial chain R&D, production capacity, 5-year warranty, and long-term spare parts supply as equity contribution.

Equipment, technology & 5-year warranty as equity
Bears operational risks jointly with Party A
27.5% dividends pre-payback / 47.5% post-payback
Party C

Cross-border Coordinator

Coordinates cross-border investment, government-enterprise relations, digital charging platform development, and long-term O&M services.

Platform development & business coordination
No operational risk or debt liability
Fixed 5% after-tax net profit dividend permanently
Project Vision

Electrifying Ethiopia's Transportation Future

Ethiopia's 10-Year Development Plan and climate commitments position the country as a leader in green mobility across East Africa. This project delivers the critical charging backbone needed to accelerate EV adoption nationwide.

  • Full-chain R&D and production from leading Chinese EV charging manufacturers including BYD, XCharge, and Star Charge
  • Digital charging platform with user app, management back-end, load dispatching, and remote equipment monitoring
  • Step dividend structure ensuring investor protection and long-term profit sharing between all parties
Implementation

Project Roadmap

Strategic phased deployment from pilot stations in Addis Ababa to nationwide coverage and local manufacturing.

Q3
Foundation & Agreements
Signing of all complete cooperation agreements, joint venture SPV registration in Ethiopia, legal framework establishment, and intermediary service agreements.
2026 — Quarter 3
I
Phase I — Pilot & Nationwide Rollout
Deploy 2,800–4,200 charging piles across 700+ existing gas station sites. Launch pilot stations in Addis Ababa in 2026, then promote nationwide rollout in 2027.
2026 – 2027
II
Phase II — Urban & Highway Expansion
Add 300–500 new charging stations in shopping malls, industrial parks, residential communities, and highway service areas across Ethiopia.
2028 – 2030
LT
Long-term — Local Production & V2G
Establish local charging pile production line, deploy energy storage cascade utilization and vehicle-grid interaction (V2G) services, and replicate the model across East Africa.
2031 – 2035
Green Growth

Sustainable Development Focus

Aligned with Ethiopia's climate resilience goals and green economy transition.

Carbon Neutrality Alignment

All charging infrastructure powered by Ethiopia's abundant hydro, wind, and solar resources. A true zero-emission transport ecosystem from generation to wheel.

Comprehensive training programs for local O&M teams, engineering roles, and manufacturing positions as production lines localize by 2031.

Financial Structure

Investor-Protective Dividend Model

The project employs a two-stage step dividend mechanism that prioritizes capital recovery for the cash-contributing party before shifting to equal profit sharing.

Party Pre-Payback Post-Payback
Party A (Local Operator) 67.5% 47.5%
Party B (Tech Supplier) 27.5% 47.5%
Party C (Coordinator) Fixed 5% Fixed 5%

Payback node verified by jointly entrusted CMA-certified audit institution. Party C bears no operational risk.
