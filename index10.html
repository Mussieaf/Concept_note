🌿Transforming Africa Through Green Innovation

Building a Sustainable Future Together


Join African First Ladies, policymakers, and leaders in a global partnership to synchronize environmental stewardship with economic empowerment. The forum will scale Ethiopia's Green Legacy Initiative, showcase clean energy technologies, and empower women and youth as agents of green transformation, uniting leaders to integrate climate-smart entrepreneurship with poverty alleviation.

🎯Forum Objectives

▼
🌱
Green Legacy Initiative ▼

Join Ethiopia's transformative movement that has planted over 47.5 billion seedlings, creating green economic opportunities and environmental resilience across the nation. This initiative serves as a model for continent-wide reforestation efforts.

💡
Clean Energy Solutions ▼

Explore innovative technologies including hydrogen-enhanced engines, improved cooking stoves, and solar systems designed for sustainable development in African contexts. These solutions reduce emissions while creating economic opportunities.

🌍
Global Partnership ▼

Unite First Ladies from Africa, Middle East, and Asia to develop coordinated emission reduction and poverty alleviation strategies. Build lasting partnerships between governments, NGOs, and private sector entities.

🤝
Women Empowerment ▼

Empower women and youth as agents of green transformation through climate-smart agriculture, entrepreneurship training, and leadership development programs tailored for sustainable impact.

African First Ladies' Initiative: A Concise Overview

Scheduled for mid-March 2026, this forum mobilizes African First Ladies and multilateral UN organizations to champion climate-smart development. It focuses on reducing emissions and poverty through the adoption of improved Cooking Stoves, Solar Devices, and Hydrogen-Enhanced Internal Combustion Engine (H-ICE) technology.

Solar Power Devices

Walker Solar Bakery, launched in 2023 by Karabo Motsamai in Maseru, Lesotho, is a pioneering, eco-friendly business that uses specialized parabolic solar cookers to bake fresh bread. Utilizing over 300 sunny days per year, it provides affordable, zero-emission, and sustainable food options to the community while training youth in green, off-grid cooking technology.

H₂-Enhanced Engines

Next-generation clean transport and power generation solutions reducing emissions by up to 46%. Our Hydrogen-Enhanced Internal Combustion Engine (H-ICE) technology integrates on-demand hydrogen generation with traditional diesel and petrol engines, significantly improving fuel efficiency while dramatically reducing harmful exhaust emissions for trucks, buses, and generators.

▼
Globo Hydro Power™ (GHP™) is an Australian company that provides a patented hydrogen fuel enhancement system designed to upgrade traditional diesel, petrol, and LPG engines into more efficient, lower-emission hydrogen hybrid engines. Their technology is primarily used in heavy vehicles (trucks, buses), marine vessels, and power generators to reduce fuel consumption by up to 45% and decrease greenhouse gas emissions.
Globo hydro power kits improve heavy-duty truck efficiency and cut emissions. Hydrogen tech boosts performance, offering cleaner energy for transport and logistics, advancing sustainability and vehicle reliability.

❓Frequently Asked Questions

▼
Who can attend the forum?

The forum is open to government delegates, UN agencies, private sector partners, NGOs, researchers, and invited guests. Registration is required for all attendees.

Is there a registration fee?

No registration fee. Sponsorship is encouraged to support the forum's sustainability goals.

Will sessions be livestreamed?

Yes, plenary sessions will be broadcast live with simultaneous translation in English, French, and Arabic. Registered virtual attendees receive full access.

What is the dress code?

Business formal for all sessions. Traditional African attire is encouraged for the opening and closing ceremonies.

How can I become a speaker?

Speaker applications closed on February 20, 2026. For late inquiries, contact mussie@equaltradeglobal.org with your proposed topic and bio.
