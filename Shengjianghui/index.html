Sharps Containers

Round and square formats from 0.5L to 30L. Puncture-resistant, leak-proof, permanent-locking design in virgin PP. Compliant with international standards; custom logo and color available.

0.5L – 30L

Clinical Waste Boxes

Reusable turnover boxes in 20L–120L with leakage-resistant structure, visible identification and warning markings. Matching consumables: waste bags, warning labels, hang tags, cable ties.

20L – 120L

Facility Waste Bins

Pedal and wheeled bins from 15L to 240L, color-coded for waste management. Heavy-duty construction, hygienic hands-free operation, multiple sizes and colors.

15L – 240L

Consumables & More

Specimen containers (urine cups, stool cups, sample tubes), medical waste bags, instrument trays, disinfection bottles and IV accessories — sterile and non-sterile options.
