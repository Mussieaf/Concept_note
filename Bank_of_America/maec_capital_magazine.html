SBLC Credit Enhancement

MAEC exists to provide alternative finance through credit enhancement of poorly rated banks which would hardly get 5% LTV if they wrote such SBLCs on an ordinary day.

MAEC gives 100% money to match the SBLC face value, minus our 6% business fees that we utilise for insurance through Lloyds and Redbridge and similar AAA insurers.

  • No cash blocking required from the issuer
  • Loan grace period of 3–5 years
  • Repayment period of 8–30 years
  • Interest at 1% per annum
  • Indemnified against all issuer risk
  • Loan repayments from produce sales and exports
  • MAEC pays the bank in 10 banking days
  • MAEC starts disbursements to project in 30 working days
