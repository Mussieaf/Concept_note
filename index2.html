African First Ladies Initiative for Emission Reduction and Poverty Alleviation Forum

Championing Clean Energy for Sustainable Development Through Women and Youth
📅 March 17-19, 2026 | The Grand Palace | Addis Ababa, Ethiopia

Transforming Africa Through Green Innovation

Building a Sustainable Future Together


Join African First Ladies, policymakers, and leaders in a global partnership to synchronize environmental stewardship with economic empowerment. The forum will scale Ethiopia’s Green Legacy Initiative, showcase clean energy technologies, and empower women and youth as agents of green transformation, uniting leaders to integrate climate-smart entrepreneurship with poverty alleviation.

🎯Forum Objectives

Green Legacy Initiative

Join Ethiopia's transformative movement that has planted over 47.5 billion seedlings, creating green economic opportunities and environmental resilience.

Clean Energy Solutions

Explore innovative technologies including hydrogen-enhanced engines, improved cooking stoves, and solar systems for sustainable development.

Global Partnership

Unite First Ladies from Africa, Middle East, and Asia to develop coordinated emission reduction and poverty alleviation strategies.

Women Empowerment

Empower women and youth as agents of green transformation through climate-smart agriculture and entrepreneurship.

Keynote Speakers

Dr. Weidong ZHANG

Zhang Weidong, Assistant Resident Representative at UNDP China, is a driving force behind innovation, partnerships, and tech-driven development. Previously a Programme Manager, his work focuses on SDG innovation, AI policy, climate action, and gender equality. He holds advanced degrees from Stanford University and Beijing Foreign Studies University.

Dr. Mena Mofed Sabet Gharsa

Dr. Gharsa, Chairman of the Board of Directors at TOP WORLD TRUST FOUNDATIONS, is an eye surgeon and scientist with over 30 years of executive leadership in global finance. As CEO of multinational investment firms, he manages substantial assets and directs project finance for infrastructure, petrochemical complexes, and renewable energy. He is also a key figure in international development, funding economic deficit settlements and humanitarian projects, with a focus on combating global warming.

Guest Speakers

Elaine Johns

Elaine Johns, Managing Director of Globo Hydro Power Pty Ltd, is a leader driving economic, sociological, and environmental advancement. She is the first female Club Director of the Enfield Returned Services League Club and the first female NSW Returned Services League Clubs Association Counselor. Recognized with awards such as the 1990 Women of the Year Award and the Who's Who Global Award, she now focuses on the global distribution of Globo Hydro Power's fuel-saving and environmental protection technology.

Rev. Dolly Nengushe

Executive Chairperson, Alliance of Christian Churches Namibia (ACCN). Reverend Dolly Nengushe is a Namibian faith leader and community development advocate focused on inclusive development, ethical leadership, and social cohesion. Her work advances women’s empowerment, youth development, and poverty alleviation, highlighting the vital role of faith-based institutions in strengthening community resilience and supporting sustainable social transformation.
