🌿Transforming Africa Through Green Innovation

Building a Sustainable Future Together


Join African First Ladies, policymakers, and leaders in a global partnership to synchronize environmental stewardship with economic empowerment. The forum will scale Ethiopia's Green Legacy Initiative, showcase clean energy technologies, and empower women and youth as agents of green transformation, uniting leaders to integrate climate-smart entrepreneurship with poverty alleviation.

African First Ladies' Initiative: A Concise Overview

Scheduled for mid-March 2026, this forum mobilizes African First Ladies and multilateral UN organizations to champion climate-smart development. It focuses on reducing emissions and poverty through the adoption of improved Cooking Stoves, Solar Devices, and Hydrogen-Enhanced Internal Combustion Engine (H-ICE) technology.

Who can attend the forum?

The forum is open to government delegates, UN agencies, private sector partners, NGOs, researchers, and invited guests. Registration is required for all attendees.

Is there a registration fee?

No registration fee. Sponsorship is encouraged.

Will sessions be livestreamed?

Yes, plenary sessions will be broadcast live with simultaneous translation in English, French, and Arabic. Registered virtual attendees receive full access.

What is the dress code?

Business formal for all sessions. Traditional African attire is encouraged for the opening and closing ceremonies.

How can I become a speaker?

Speaker applications closed on February 20, 2026. For late inquiries, contact mussie@equaltradeglobal.org with your proposed topic and bio.
