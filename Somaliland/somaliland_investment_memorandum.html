Executive Overview

Credibility Beyond Recognition

Somaliland stands at the gateway of the Red Sea trade corridor — a stable democracy linking Africa and the Gulf. Despite limited formal diplomatic recognition, it has sustained over thirty years of peace, self-governance, and adherence to the rule of law; a record unmatched in the Horn of Africa.

01

Strategic Road Corridor

183km climate-resilient asphalt highway linking Hargeisa, Oodweyne, and Burao — reducing logistics costs by 25–35% and unlocking livestock and mineral trade corridors.

02

Renewable Energy & Grid

Utility-scale solar, coastal wind farms, and battery storage systems to displace diesel dependence, lower tariffs, and power industrial productivity across the nation.

03

Water Supply Expansion

30 million litres/day immediate expansion for Hargeisa, with borehole development, transmission pipelines, and smart metering to secure urban water sustainability.

National Infrastructure Programmes

Three Pillars of Transformation

Each project is structured to produce measurable economic returns, reduce operating costs across the economy, and expand the fiscal base through asset-backed, revenue-linked financing.

🛣️

Strategic Road Corridor

183km · Hargeisa – Oodweyne – Burao
Programme 01

Connecting the Economic Heartland

This corridor links the administrative capital, livestock trade hubs, and major inland economic zones. Current road conditions inflate vehicle operating costs, travel time, and transport losses. Upgrading to a climate-resilient asphalt highway reduces logistics cost per ton and improves export competitiveness.

Technical scope includes full asphalt pavement with engineered load capacity, drainage and flood resilience systems, bridge construction, road safety infrastructure, axle-load control stations, and a 10–15 year maintenance contract.

Renewable Energy & Grid

Solar · Wind · BESS · Grid Modernisation
Programme 02

Powering the Next Economy

Electricity generation is predominantly diesel-based, leading to high tariff levels, fuel import exposure, and grid instability. The proposed energy mix introduces utility-scale solar farms, coastal wind farms, and Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) to fundamentally restructure the national energy architecture.

Expected impact includes reduced fuel import bills, lower average tariffs over the medium term, improved industrial productivity, increased formal electricity billing revenue, and micro-grids for rural electrification.

Hargeisa Water Supply

30M Litres/Day · Phase I & II
Programme 03

Securing Urban Water Futures

Current per capita supply is below sustainable urban benchmarks, constraining public health and urban growth. Phase I delivers immediate stabilisation through 30 million litres/day expansion, borehole development, 23.5km transmission pipeline, and storage reservoirs.

Phase II advances surface water harvesting, smart metering, leakage reduction, and solar-powered pumping to create a fully modernised, efficient water utility serving Somaliland's growing capital.

Security & Collateral Framework

Multi-Layer Credit Enhancement

Asset-backed structuring lowers perceived sovereign risk through ring-fenced revenue allocations, independent audit oversight, and conservative loan-to-value caps.

Port Revenue-Linked Structure

Debt service supported through defined allocation of port revenues held in an offshore escrow account with priority payment waterfall.

  • Gross revenue collection
  • Operational cost deduction
  • Debt service priority payment
  • Surplus government allocation

Mineral-Backed Credit Enhancement

Mineral assets structured under independent geological certification with conservative pricing and diversified portfolio approach.

  • Gold & Rare Earth Prospective Zones
  • Gypsum & Limestone Deposits
  • Copper & Iron Ore Resources
  • Royalty assignment & Export receivables pledge
Risk Analysis

Mitigated & Managed

Every risk category is addressed through structural, legal, and institutional mechanisms designed to protect lender interests and ensure project viability.

01

Political Risk

Mitigated by 34+ years of uninterrupted constitutional governance, six peaceful presidential transitions, and no military coups or foreign peacekeeping presence.

02

Revenue Risk

Mitigated through escrow arrangements, multiple revenue streams, ring-fenced port allocations, and project-linked SPV revenues.

03

Commodity Risk

Mitigated through conservative mineral pricing assumptions, diversified resource base, and independent geological certification.

04

Construction & Governance Risk

Mitigated via EPC contracts, performance guarantees, independent annual audits, transparent procurement, and international arbitration clauses.
